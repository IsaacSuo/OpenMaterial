================================================================================
  KEY DISCOVERIES
================================================================================

DISCOVERY #1: DUAL NORMAL SYSTEM
- GaussianShader uses _normal and _normal2 (view-dependent selection)
- Your single normal is actually BETTER for 2D objects
- Recommendation: KEEP YOUR SINGLE NORMAL APPROACH (simpler, sufficient)

DISCOVERY #2: DIFFUSE REPRESENTATION
- GaussianShader mixes diffuse in _features_dc (view-dependent)
- Your separate albedo parameter is CLEANER
- Recommendation: KEEP YOUR SEPARATE ALBEDO (better semantics)

DISCOVERY #3: DEPTH-BASED NORMAL SUPERVISION
- GaussianShader uses rendered depth to compute pseudo-GT normals
- SELF-SUPERVISED (no external models like Marigold needed)
- Naturally aligns with rendering geometry
- Recommendation: ADOPT THIS (your plan mentioned Marigold as option)

DISCOVERY #4: ALPHA REGULARIZATION
- GaussianShader uses zero_one_loss to encourage binary opacity
- Simple but CRITICAL for clean geometry (eliminates "fog")
- YOUR PLAN IS MISSING THIS - MUST ADD!
- Recommendation: IMPLEMENT zero_one_loss immediately

DISCOVERY #5: LEARNING RATE HIERARCHY
- Environment map learning rate: 0.016 (very high)
- Material learning rates: 0.0001 (very low)
- Ratio: 160x difference!
- GaussianShader's tuning works well
- Recommendation: USE THEIR LR STRATEGY

DISCOVERY #6: NORMAL INITIALIZATION
- GaussianShader starts normals at ZERO (trained from scratch)
- Roughness initialized to 0.6 (moderate, not shiny)
- Metallic initialized to -2.0 (sigmoid -> 0.1, non-metallic)
- This works better than random init
- Recommendation: ADOPT THIS INITIALIZATION STRATEGY

================================================================================
  ACTIVATION FUNCTIONS & INITIALIZATION
================================================================================

Activation Functions:
├─ Albedo: sigmoid (maps R to [0, 1])
├─ Metallic: sigmoid (maps R to [0, 1])
├─ Roughness: sigmoid + bias + clamp (enforce min 0.1, avoid too-shiny)
└─ Normal: unit norm (post-process after learning)

Initialization Values:
├─ Albedo: From point cloud colors (logit for inverse sigmoid)
├─ Roughness: zeros (sigmoid(0) = 0.5 in output space)
├─ Metallic: -2.0 (sigmoid(-2) ≈ 0.12, non-metallic default)
└─ Normal: zeros (trained from pseudo-GT supervision)

================================================================================
  CRITICAL GOTCHAS
================================================================================

GOTCHA #1: BRDF LUT Dependency
Problem: Split-Sum approximation requires pre-computed BRDF LUT
Solution: Use analytical BRDF or load GaussianShader's file
Timeline: Decide in Week 1

GOTCHA #2: Learning Rate Sensitivity
Problem: Wrong LRs cause divergence or no convergence
Solution: GaussianShader's 100x ratio works; don't deviate initially
Timeline: Verify in Week 4

GOTCHA #3: Coordinate System Alignment
Problem: Depth->normal conversion depends on camera calibration
Solution: Double-check camera K, R, T in your data loader
Timeline: Test in Week 2

GOTCHA #4: Normal Supervision Timing
Problem: Normal loss might compete with geometry learning early on
Solution: Start normal supervision immediately (GaussianShader does this)
Timeline: Test different schedules in Week 3

GOTCHA #5: Boundary Artifacts
Problem: Loss computed on half-transparent pixels causes noise
Solution: Erode alpha mask before loss weighting (4-pixel erosion)
Timeline: Implement in Week 3 loss functions

GOTCHA #6: SH vs Separable Albedo
Problem: Mixing view-dependent SH with PBR albedo is incoherent
Solution: Either keep SH as pure residual OR use separate albedo
Recommendation: Use separate albedo (cleaner)
Timeline: Decision in Week 1
